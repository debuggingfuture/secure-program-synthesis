# Postern — 10-line summary

1. **Problem.** Per-source authorization is *non-compositional* under ETL fusion: when heterogeneous sources are materialized into a single lakehouse, per-source guards (channel ACLs, field-level security, OAuth scopes) have no representative in the lake's authorization surface, so the querying agent inherits the *union* of upstream permissions rather than the *intersection* under its own identity.
2. **Thesis.** Insert a verified gateway that *rewrites* every plan against a small column-grant policy; agents and their generated SQL stay untrusted.
3. **Lean 4 spec.** `Plan = Scan | Project | Filter(φ) | Join | Aggregate` with `φ : Pred` a predicate term (`Ref c | Lit v | App op args`), column-grant `Policy` + `AggGrant` (abstract DP boundary), `rewrite : ... → Option Plan` with explicit refusals.
4. **Headline theorems.** `rewrite_sound` (output-column ⊆ allowed), `rewrite_filter_sound` (predicate-column ⊆ allowed) and its pointwise φ-level restatement `rewrite_filter_coverage` (every free col of every Filter predicate ⊆ allowed) — together close the `WHERE ssn = ?` side-channel for compound predicates too.
5. **Proof discipline.** Thirteen theorems; ten fully `sorry`-free, three with `sorryAx` only on the `Join` arm (non-Join cases proved). `CheckAxioms.lean` confirms the axiom set is bounded by `{propext, Quot.sound}` (Lean stdlib).
6. **Rust mirror.** `postern-core` reimplements the same types and `rewrite` algorithm for a Polars / DuckDB gateway.
7. **Conformance testing.** Lean emits a JSON corpus of 31 cases; `postern-diff` runs Rust against it. **31 / 31 pass** — 19 accept, 12 refuse (filter side-channels, unknown relation, join-key leak, inadmissible aggregate, forbidden group-by, compound predicate with one forbidden ref).
8. **Demo.** Kaggle `transactions-fraud-datasets`, three principals (CRM / CardOps / FraudRisk) — PII redaction, cross-department refusal, minimum-necessary disclosure.
9. **Repro.** `scripts/reproduce.sh` runs Lean → axiom audit → corpus → Rust → diff end-to-end in under two minutes.
10. **Open challenges.** Join-arm idempotence/monotonicity/cross-leg filter (3 sorries); concrete DP mechanism plugged into the `aggAllowed` boundary; the mutual-information half of the filter side-channel (value-channel inference through allowed columns); biscuit attenuation modelled inside the proof (paper §6). Defense-in-depth: pair with agent-side capability tracking [Odersky et al. 2026, [arXiv:2603.00991](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00991)] — the two layers compose without re-verifying each other's TCB.
